# Provenance of committed results

The `results.csv`/`results.json`, baseline tables, plots and selection manifest are **historical Laya medium-profile results from 2026-10-09**, not results of the GLiNER ↔ Laya experiment. GLiNER P0 audit files under `outputs/experiments/2026-10-09-gliner-p0/` establish runtime feasibility and tiny train–save–reload mechanics only; the tiny adapter predicted ABSTAIN for every sampled state. The project implementation status is recorded separately in `.claude/plans/2026-10-09-gliner-laya-implementation.md`.

`test_strict` is a **reused/exposed holdout**, not a new untouched confirmatory test. The historical selection used a 64-episode validation probe; do not infer full-validation optimality from the selection scores. Historical E05 (Laya RLCD masked argmax) answered zero questions on both splits: its reward is not evidence of QA quality. E07 was skipped. These results do not demonstrate a GLiNER improvement or a completed P0–P7 rerun.

Only small aggregate outputs and audit metadata are committed. No raw questions/contexts, dataset files, model weights, adapter weights, predictions or live cumulative GPU ledger are included. Source-data redistribution permission is unverified. Some original generated JSON/CSV records contain absolute paths from the original machine; their hashes and numeric fields have been left unchanged. Refer to the matching relative path within this repository when checking local artifacts.

The historical `REPORT.md`, `REPORT.txt`, `MODEL_CARD.md`, `FINAL_HANDOFF.md`, `REPRODUCE.md`, `artifact_manifest.sha256` and `handoff_metadata.zip` remain local/untracked: the generated reports contain empty strict-split counts and incomplete holdout wording; three entries in the artifact manifest do not currently verify, and the zip duplicates stale material. Do not treat those excluded files as committed or validated reports. Plots are historical diagnostics only. GLiNER gate logs/checkpoints and the complete environment remain local; the small gate summaries and pinned dependency list are included for audit, not for claiming reproducibility without the upstream data/model and licensed access.
